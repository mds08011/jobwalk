How it works
  • Load the sheet, drop your photos, then: the highlighted photo in the list is "armed" — click the sheet where it was taken to place its pin. The next unpinned photo arms automatically, so you can go click-click-click down the walk.
  • Drag a pin to adjust it. Click a pin to see the photo. "unpin" in the list removes a pin.
  • Plan sheet PDFs work directly (pick the page). For a plan area from a bigger sheet, a screenshot (Win+Shift+S) pasted into a file works too.
  • Captions typed in the list show up in the pin popups of the exported file.
  • The downloaded file is fully self-contained and works with no internet at all — no basemap needed.

Everything runs locally in your browser — no data is uploaded anywhere.
