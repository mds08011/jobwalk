Tips & troubleshooting
  • Captions typed in the photo list flow into the map popups, the photo log, and the KMZ.
  • No GPS tag? The photo still goes in the photo log, just not on the map. Location must be on for the camera app (iPhone: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Camera → While Using).
  • HEIC photos can't be read by the browser. On iPhone set Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible, or transfer via OneDrive / cable (usually converts to JPEG and keeps the GPS tag). Texting or some AirDrop paths strip GPS tags.
  • Measure: the ruler button on the map (here and in exported maps) measures distance in feet and area in acres — click points, double-click to finish, Esc to cancel.
  • All downloads are self-contained — email them or drop them in the job folder. Internet is only needed to draw the basemap. The photo log opens in a browser and prints to PDF (Ctrl+P); captions in it stay editable until printed.
  • Photos are numbered in the order taken; numbers match between map dots, photo log, and KMZ.

Everything runs locally in your browser — no photo data is uploaded anywhere.
